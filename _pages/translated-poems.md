### On Living by Nâzım Hikmet

([here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vzU-KsyJkFA) is a music composition of this poem by Fazıl Say)

(I)<br>
Living is no laughing matter,<br>
You'll live with great seriousness,<br>
Like a squirrel for instance,<br>
That is, without expecting anything outside and beyond life,<br>
That is, your sole purpose will be to live.<br>
<br>
You'll take living seriously,<br>
To such a degree, so much so that,<br>
For instance, your arms tied behind your back, your back against the wall,<br>
Or with your huge glasses,<br>
In your white shirt in a laboratory,<br>
You'll be able to die for people.<br>
Even for those whose faces you haven't even seen,<br>
Even if nobody has forced you into it,<br>
Even though you know that the most real thing is to live.<br>
<br>
So, you'll take living so seriously that,<br>
Even in your seventies, for instance, you'll plant olives,<br>
Not to leave it to the children or anything like that,<br>
But because although you’re afraid of dying, you don’t believe in death,<br>
Because living outweighs.<br>
<br>
(II)<br>
Let's say, we're a patient in critical condition,<br>
So, from the white table<br>
There's a chance of never waking up again too,<br>
Although it’s impossible not to feel the sorrow of leaving a little early,<br>
We'll nevertheless laugh at the jokes of Bektasi,<br>
We'll look out of the window, to see if it's raining,<br>
Or we'll still wait impatiently<br>
For the latest news bulletin.<br>
<br>
Let's say, for something worth fighting for,<br>
Let's say we're on the frontline.<br>
Right there, in the first attack, on that very day,<br>
There's a chance of falling flat on our face and dying too.<br>
We'll know this with a strange sense of resentment,<br>
And yet we'll still be madly curious<br>
For that which may take years, the end of war.<br>
<br>
Let's say, we're in prison,<br>
Our age is around fifty,<br>
And we still have eighteen years until the iron door opens,<br>
Nevertheless, we'll live with the outside,<br>
With its people, animals, fights and winds<br>
That is, the outside behind the wall.<br>
<br>
So, whenever and wherever we are,<br>
Living like we'll never die...<br>
<br>
(III)<br>
This world will grow cold,<br>
A star among the stars,<br>
One of the tiniest ones even,<br>
A star particle on a blue velvet<br>
That is, this enormous world of ours.<br>
<br>
This world will grow cold one day,<br>
Not like a pack of ice,<br>
Or a dead cloud either,<br>
It'll roll like an empty walnut,<br>
In the pitch black darkness, vast and endless.<br>
<br>
Even now, the pain of it must sting,<br>
The sorrow has to be felt already.<br>
This world must be loved like that<br>
For you to say "I have lived"...<br>
